# Maternity Pay Calculator UK — Statutory Maternity Pay Week by Week

> Statutory Maternity Pay is 90% of your earnings for 6 weeks, then the lower of £194.32 a week and 90% for 33 weeks.

Reference notes for the UK tax year **2026/27**, computed from HMRC's
published rates rather than copied from another site.

## How it works

The full 39 weeks, in two stages:

| Weeks | Rate |
|---|---|
| 1 to 6 | 90% of average weekly earnings, no cap |
| 7 to 39 | lower of £194.32 and 90% of earnings |
| 40 to 52 | unpaid, unless your employer offers more |

SMP is taxable and National Insurance is due on it, so the figure that lands is lower again.

The 90% is based on earnings in a specific eight-week reference period before the qualifying week, which is why a pay rise or a quiet month just before can change the whole 39 weeks.

## What this does not cover

Contractual maternity pay from your employer is often more generous and usually replaces rather than adds to SMP. Check your policy before planning around statutory rates.

## Source and tax year

Figures are for the 2026/27 tax year, which runs 6 April 2026
to 5 April 2027, and were checked against
[gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027) on 28 August 2026.

Rates change every April, and often at a Budget. Check the tax year a figure
belongs to before relying on it.
